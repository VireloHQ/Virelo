  One 6-second clip (working-file offset 00:12) and one 10-second clip

  the other uses center framing.

The demo records the current, unmodified Virelo Shorts interface, a favorite
action and native vertical scrolling through these three clips with an isolated
demonstration catalog. It returns to the first clip before looping.
The final recording is resized to 390 x 820 and encoded as silent H.264
at 30 fps. The poster is a frame captured from the same Virelo UI.
No full movie, third-party soundtrack or downloaded social-media footage
is included. This demonstration is not a movie catalogue supplied by Virelo.
